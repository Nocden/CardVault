Your SOL, locked to a physical card

Money in a CardVault moves only when your wallet and your card both sign. Malware that steals your wallet key gets nothing without the card and its PIN.

Connect Phantom to start. Use devnet: Phantom settings, Developer settings, Testnet mode.

Create your vault

You need two cards: the main card for payments and a recovery card kept somewhere safe. The recovery card can move the vault to a new wallet if your wallet key is lost or stolen.
